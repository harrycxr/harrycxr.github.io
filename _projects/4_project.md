### Project Overview
Supported by the **National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) Youth Program** (国家自然科学基金青年科学基金项目 (C类), Grant No. `52201401`, 2023.01 – 2025.12).

### Background & Challenges
Underwater sensor networks frequently encounter extreme measurement outliers caused by biological acoustic interference, surface reflections, and fluctuating ambient ocean noise. Classical statistical estimators often diverge when noise distributions deviate from Gaussian assumptions.

### Key Innovations & Methodology
1. **Quantum Optimization Framework**:
   - Developed a robust optimization paradigm inspired by quantum annealing and variational quantum eigensolvers to address high-dimensional, multi-modal localization objective functions.
2. **Data Cleansing & Spatiotemporal Feature Engineering**:
   - Formulated spatiotemporal outlier rejection algorithms to scrub corrupted acoustic time-series data.
   - Designed robust feature extraction pipelines to ensure consistent tracking performance even under severely degraded signal-to-noise ratios (SNR).
