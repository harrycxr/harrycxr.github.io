## About Me

I am currently a Master's student in **Traffic Information Engineering and Control** at **Shanghai Maritime University** (Rank: 1/45, GPA: 90.8/100). I am a core member of the **Smart Deep Blue IoT Team** (Shanghai High-level Discipline Construction Project / 智慧深蓝物联网团队 · 上海市高水平学科建设项目) and a **Student Member** of the **Operations Research Society of China** (中国优选法统筹法与经济数学研究会). Prior to this, I received my B.Eng. degree in **Software Engineering** from **East China Jiaotong University** in 2023 (Rank: 4/63, GPA: 88.1/100).

My primary research interests focus on **Underwater Unmanned System (UUS)**, **Multi-Source Information Fusion**, **Adaptive Robust State Estimation**, and **Perception-Planning & Control**.

---

## Research Interests

- **Localization & Tracking of Marine Vehicles**: Nonlinear filtering theory on Riemannian manifolds; adaptive robust state estimation under intermittent packet loss and measurement attenuation.
- **Uncertain Systems & Clustered Sensor Network Fusion**: Parallel/sequential covariance intersection (CI) steady-state robust filtering; two-level decentralized fusion architectures.
- **Theoretical Lower Bounds for Estimation**: Kernelized Intrinsic McAulay-Seidman Bound (K-IMSB) for nonlinear estimation problems.
- **Observer & Controller Design under Complex Disturbances**: Bi-level spatio-temporal state observers with selective state spaces; energy-efficient nonlinear model predictive control (NMPC).
